# Concise Main-Thread Explanation

Read for paper-simple-explain. Produce a shorter, self-contained expert account of the study's important research logic, not a miniature complete-methods manuscript, an abstract paraphrase or disconnected takeaway bullets.

## Select the core story

Use original sources and a valid research model to identify the main question, relevant prior capability/gap, central approach, decisive results and important limits. Verify the source sections, methods and supporting evidence needed for those claims. Reuse previously checked material when current; do not demand a prior full-detail run or write its exhaustive manuscript first. If essential sources are missing, explain the resulting bounded scope rather than inventing a complete story. Do not mark the whole source inventory read or the full-detail stage complete merely because the concise account is ready.

Follow the paper's actual discipline and evidence. A theoretical account needs its core assumptions and proof idea; a computational account its representation/procedure and evaluation; an empirical account its intervention/measurement, controls and inference. Keep the details whose omission would materially change the meaning or credibility of the central finding. Routine parameters, all panels, secondary ablations and long derivations can remain outside this requested scope unless needed for the main inference.

## Write a short connected account

Apply [topic flow and motivation](topic-flow.md). Usually deliver the complete main story in one response using three to five informative small-topic headings with short paragraphs. Respect an explicit segmented preference or requested length, with independent progress if continuation is needed. Briefly state the appropriate expert lens, then make the gap's practical consequence clear before naming the solution.

For each stage, state what we are trying to explain, why the previous issue/result requires it and the relevant paper anchor; normally fit that lead into one sentence. Develop the reason, approach, evidence and consequence rather than listing section summaries. Explain why the main design choice addresses the problem and why the comparison is informative. Use the result to motivate the next stage. Close with what the paper establishes, its main significance and the most consequential uncertainty or limitation.

Explain necessary vocabulary locally and give the verified English name beside every important translated mention, including repeated/bold terms. Use a minimal prerequisite explanation where needed, not an unexplained acronym to save words. A central calculation may need its key operation and interpretation; do not show every derivation step by default. If a topic cannot be made shorter without losing a premise, refine the wording/scope or explain the needed step; do not make a misleading shortcut. Relevant supporting-source topics carry the same parenthetical required/optional labels as detail mode.

No prior chat, figure lesson or map is needed to follow this account. History can supply a proportionate optional personal connection only after the core topic is explained. Use complete-source knowledge to choose important content rather than stitching together old answers. A concise explanation intentionally covers the central research chain, not every substantive paper section as full detail does.

## Save and distinguish completion

Save the concise text, core source crosswalk, important claims/terms, optional supporting topics, limitations/gaps and delivered scope under progress.simple, separately from progress.detail and progress.figures. A complete concise account means its selected main research chain was explained and delivered. It does not mark translation, all figures, every method, a full-detail manuscript or reader understanding complete. Reuse the same paper settings, expert profile, sources and stable map entry. Changes in meaningful understanding can update that map through the existing conditional review; brevity alone is not a reason to redraw it.
