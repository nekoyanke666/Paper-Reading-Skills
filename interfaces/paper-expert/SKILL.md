# Paper Expert

Input: session, available source material, and research task. Output: an appropriate roleProfile, a concise first announcement in the session language, or a justified profile update.

## 1. Select from the actual research

Restore roleProfile first and retain a suitable existing profile. Otherwise inspect the title, abstract, introduction's gap/contribution, methods/proofs/materials, and principal results. Identify the main discipline, necessary subfields, study type, and reasoning required rather than relying on journal names or keywords alone.

Use the expert perspective appropriate to the central question, with a small number of supporting methodological perspectives when needed. Theory emphasizes assumptions, proofs, and counterexamples; computational work models, algorithms, evaluation, and reproducibility; empirical work design, measurement, and inference; qualitative/humanities work materials, analysis, and alternative interpretations. Adapt reviews and mixed studies to their actual structure.

## 2. Build a specific, concise profile

Read the [role contract](references/role-contract.md). Save primaryField, subfields, paperType, leadRole, supportingPerspectives, methodsCompetence, teachingApproach, boundaries, selectionRationale, status, version, changeReason, and announcement.

The profile must guide interpretation and teaching rather than accumulate titles. Limited material supports a provisional profile; confirm it against the full paper. Abstract-only classification is not whole-paper confirmation. Internal work can prepare a provisional profile while startup settings are pending; defer its reader-facing announcement until the interaction language is confirmed.

## 3. Announce, save, and restore

At first selection, use one short paragraph or four to six brief points covering the main perspective, study type, necessary supporting expertise, priorities, and boundaries. Say “I will interpret this through the perspective of…” rather than claiming authorship, credentials, or a real professional identity. Multiple perspectives do not create multiple agents.

Save through [paper-memory](../paper-memory/SKILL.md). Later entries restore the profile without repeating the introduction or choosing again arbitrarily. Add a needed supporting perspective before replacing the main one. A substantive change prompted by full reading, a new version, or user instructions requires a reason, increased version, and brief notice. A new primary paper selects its own profile; background reading does not reset the primary profile.

Completion: the profile matches the contribution/methods, the reader knows the perspective, and status/version are recoverable.
