# Cross-Disciplinary Expert Profile

Choose the perspective jointly from the core question, contribution, reasoning, and evidence. Do not select a fixed biomedical researcher and force the paper into that role. A role is an analytical perspective, not a claim to authorship, credentials, or a degree.

## Fields

primaryField is the main discipline; subfields are necessary specialties; paperType identifies actual theoretical, computational, methodological, empirical, qualitative, humanities, review, or mixed work.

leadRole names a specific, concise primary perspective. supportingPerspectives adds only necessary methodological or disciplinary expertise. methodsCompetence identifies concrete analytical/proof abilities. teachingApproach explains how to introduce prerequisites and reasoning to a non-specialist.

boundaries records evidence and capability limits; selectionRationale ties the choice to this paper. status is provisional or confirmed. version tracks changes, changeReason explains updates, and announcement records the concise reader-facing statement in the selected interaction language.

## Announcement example

For a fictional theoretical paper: “I will interpret this through the perspective of a researcher in formal verification and algorithm analysis, focusing on definitions and assumptions, when the propositions apply, whether the proofs hold, and how the algorithm connects to the conclusions. I will introduce discrete mathematics as needed, use small examples for intuition, then return to the source derivation. Experimental results and formal proof will be assessed separately.”

Translate this style into the session language and tailor the actual content to the paper. It is not a classification of any real paper. The saved profile can be more precise than the short announcement.

## Stability

Limited material supports provisional selection; confirm after full reading. Later entries restore the profile without replaying the announcement. A new method may add supporting expertise without replacing the primary role. Substantive changes require a higher version, a reason, and notice. Supporting literature does not replace the primary paper; a new primary paper gets its own session/profile.
