# Paper Evidence

**The user's time is valuable.** Minimize unnecessary waiting: reuse valid work, load only relevant material, and avoid redundant steps while preserving the requested completeness, evidence checks and saved-state safety.

Input: claim, sources, saved expert profile, and claim index. Output: located evidence, supported scope, compatible alternatives, and claim/correction updates.

## 1. Establish the answerable scope

Confirm access to full text, figures/captions, methods, and supplements, including proofs or materials where appropriate. If only an abstract or partial text is available, state the answerable scope. Inspect what this task requires rather than restarting collection of every source.

Give verifiable locations for important paper claims, such as Figure 2b, a methods subsection, theorem, or paragraph. Never fabricate numbers, quotations, or source locations.

## 2. Separate findings, interpretation, and added knowledge

Distinguish direct observations, computations, proofs, or material analyses from author interpretations/hypotheses and from added background or your inference. Judge theoretical propositions under their assumptions and proofs rather than calling every disciplinary finding an experimental observation.

Identify external sources separately so the reader does not mistake them for this paper's findings. When information is insufficient, specify what is missing and which judgment it affects. Express the explanation in the confirmed interaction language while preserving source identifiers.

## 3. Check inference in its research context

Reconstruct the author's research logic while remaining critical. Publication in Nature or another prestigious venue is not evidence that a conclusion is correct.

Distinguish association from causality, evidence from hypothesis, and model-specific findings from their generalizable scope. Do not invent unstated motives or mistake a coherent narrative for sufficient evidence.

For empirical work check design, intervention, controls, measurement, and alternatives; for theory definitions, assumptions, proof validity, and counterexamples; for computational/methods work tasks, data, benchmarks, comparison fairness, and reproducibility; for qualitative/humanities work materials, sampling/selection, analysis, and alternative readings.

When asked whether something is really established, name the proposition, direct basis, compatible alternatives, and missing discriminating evidence. Separate necessary from sufficient conditions and empirical support from formal proof. Discuss limitations that matter to this judgment.

## 4. Handle disagreement and corrections

Recheck original sources and whether apparent conflict arises from versions, objects, conditions, measurements, or interpretations. If an error is established, identify the original statement, correction, and location; trace dependent claims, explanation, translation, and map content. If evidence remains conflicting, preserve unresolved/disputed status instead of forcing agreement.

## 5. Return the evidence index

Return important statements, kinds, status, evidence/proof locations, conditions, and limits for [paper-memory](../paper-memory/SKILL.md) to save in claims. Evidence supporting a claim is distinct from a causal/logical relationship between research objects.

Completion: important claims have traceable sources or explicit uncertainty, and corrections include their dependent effects. Reader-facing explanation follows [paper-teach](../paper-teach/SKILL.md).
