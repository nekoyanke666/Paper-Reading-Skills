# Paper Compare

**The user's time is valuable.** Minimize unnecessary waiting: reuse valid work, load only relevant material, and avoid redundant steps while preserving the requested completeness, evidence checks and saved-state safety.

Input: primary paper, comparison material, versions, and question. Output: sourced comparability, common ground, differences, conflict assessment, and remaining gaps, in the saved language.

## 1. Establish the comparison basis

Follow paper-teach and paper-evidence. Confirm versions, available material, and purpose while preserving primary, background, and comparison roles. Restrict abstract-only comparisons appropriately and obtain necessary material through paper-sources.

## 2. Check whether the studies answer the same question

Compare specific questions, objects, conditions/assumptions, interventions/arguments, measurements, analyses, and conclusion levels. Establish direct comparability before deciding whether results are mutually incompatible. Different conditions, complementary studies, or different evidence strength are not automatically contradictions.

Label unverified explanations of differences as inference rather than established fact. Use comparison criteria suited to the disciplinary evidence.

## 3. Assess novelty and conflict

For novelty, check the author's claim against actual differences from necessary prior work. If external search is needed, verify original research and state the search scope. Without adequate checking, do not claim global firstness or absence of precedent.

For conflict, use paper-evidence to revisit original basis, versions, and conditions. Preserve disagreement that cannot be resolved. Cite each study's relevant locations so the reader can identify which source supports each statement.

## 4. Return comparison results

Return criteria, comparable scope, differences, evidence strength, and gaps for paper-memory, keeping primary identity unchanged. The caller decides whether important relationships belong on the map; this interface does not add an entry or restart full guidance.
