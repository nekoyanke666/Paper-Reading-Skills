# Original Graphics, In-Figure Translation and Author Captions

Read for every figure in the requested translation range. A figure unit contains the authentic scientific image, localized in-figure text when needed, and the authors' own caption translated beside it. It does not contain a newly written assistant explanation.

## Identify and retain the actual figure

Inventory each in-scope figure with source/version, figure ID, page/region, panels and caption. Include all research figures in the requested document/range; separately downloaded Supplementary/Extended Data material follows the actual requested scope. Match captions by their source identifiers, not by proximity alone. Distinguish the author caption or figure legend from a color/group key inside the graphic; both source text types are translated, neither is replaced by a new interpretation.

Reuse publisher/user-provided original assets, or extract/render the actual figure from the source page. Inspect the full image and readable labels. Preserve panels, axes, scale bars, keys, annotations and insets. Never replace it with a recreated plot, generated illustration, analogy or placeholder. Save the authentic original unchanged with checksum/provenance or page/crop/render basis and keep it available for parallel comparison.

## Localize text while preserving the scientific graphic

Use the same data-bearing graphic in the translated document, with a separate localized text/annotation layer when necessary. Translate axis titles, key/group labels, diagram text and embedded explanatory wording. Preserve plotted marks, coordinates/scales, numbers, units, formulas, color meanings, panel letters and scientific identifiers. A target-language label may retain its original/verified English term for lookup, but must not introduce a new scientific interpretation.

Place translated labels at clearly corresponding elements. Dense graphics may use anchored callouts or a panel enlargement when replacing text in place would obscure data; record any layout deviation. A detached caption or label dictionary alone is not a completed visual translation. Use appropriate available graphics/document tools and visually compare with the original; a tool that changes data-bearing content does not produce an acceptable faithful localized figure. Mark unreadable text individually rather than guessing it.

The main document prioritizes the configured target language and source-like layout. Keep original assets/original pages available as the comparison version; do not automatically print duplicate original-plus-translated figures on the main page if that breaks its layout. Important translated labels retain adjacent verified English names under the shared every-mention rule. Wording already in the target language or a figure with no translatable text can reuse the original after inspection, with its reason recorded.

## Translate the existing caption; do not author another explanation

Use the full caption/figure legend supplied by the authors, including its title if present, panel references, measurements, controls, sample/replicate information, statistics, comparisons and qualifications. Preserve its factual content and uncertainty without summarizing or embellishing it. If the original caption does not state an observation, motivation or conclusion, do not add that statement as if it were caption text.

Keep the translated author caption with the matching image at its source-associated location. Prefer the original figure/caption grouping and placement. If separately supplied caption assets must be assembled with the figure, retain their original locators and make the correspondence explicit. Keep page/figure continuity clear across unavoidable page breaks. Do not shrink image detail or caption text to illegibility merely to force identical pagination.

No reader's guide, lesson, new purpose/result explanation or inferred conclusion is required or produced by default. Minimal translator notes may identify genuine ambiguity/illegibility, clearly outside author text; they do not become a figure-analysis section. Do not invoke a complete figure-tour workflow for caption translation or mark progress.figures as explained. User-requested teaching remains a separate entry/task.

## Save image-aware progress and verify output

Use stable keys combining source and figure identity under progress.translation.figureUnits. Record source/version/page/region, original/localized asset paths and provenance, target language/settings version, label coverage/unreadable items, caption alignment/status, presentation choice and actual output checks. There is no reader-note completion requirement.

A unit is complete only when its authentic graphic is present/readable, the required visual text is translated or inspected inapplicable, the original author caption is faithfully translated and co-located, and output legibility/asset links are checked. Preserve originals, valid text work and prior revisions on continuation; update the matching unit rather than duplicate it. An earlier text-only document can retain completed prose while overall image-aware completion stays partial until its requested figures/captions are finished. Language changes invalidate localized components as needed, not the authentic originals.
